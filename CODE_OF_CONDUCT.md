# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to maintain a technical community that is respectful, constructive, and focused on improving GIS engineering practice.

Expected behavior:

- be respectful and professional
- critique ideas and implementations, not people
- provide evidence for technical claims
- acknowledge uncertainty
- welcome different technical approaches
- keep discussions relevant to the project

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information.

Maintainers may moderate discussions or contributions that violate these standards.
