# 3D GIS Profile

The system visualizes or analyzes terrain, buildings, BIM, point clouds, 3D assets, or other spatial scenes.

- software-engineering
- 3d-geospatial
- webgis-map-engine
- gis-correctness
- performance-optimization
- webgis-testing
- webgis-security
- documentation-engineering

## Conditional Skills
- point-cloud-lidar
- postgis-engineering
- geoserver-engineering
- realtime-gis
- remote-sensing
- geoai

## Mandatory Gates
Horizontal/vertical CRS, datum, height semantics, LOD, streaming strategy, device fallback, performance, 2D/3D consistency.
